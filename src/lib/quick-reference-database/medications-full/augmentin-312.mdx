## Indication

- As for other amoxicillin/clavulanate products: infections where beta-lactamase producing organisms are suspected.

---

## Dose

- Use manufacturer-specified dosing. Typical adult regimens include 500/125 mg three times daily or 875/125 mg twice daily. Paediatric dosing is weight-based (amoxicillin component commonly 25–45 mg/kg/day divided).

---

## Administration

- Take with food to limit GI upset.

---

## Contraindications

<Alert type="error">
  - Hypersensitivity to penicillins or clavulanic acid.
</Alert>

---

## Adverse Reactions

- Gastrointestinal side effects and hypersensitivity reactions; monitor for C. difficile.

---

## Pregnancy and Lactation

- Use when indicated; consult product SmPC.
