# Celecoxib (Celebrex)

<Callout>
Selective COX-2 inhibitor NSAID.
Reduces pain and inflammation with lower risk of gastric irritation than non-selective NSAIDs.
</Callout>

---

## Indication
- Osteoarthritis
- Rheumatoid arthritis
- Ankylosing spondylitis
- Acute pain (including dental or postoperative pain)
- Dysmenorrhoea (period pain)

### Children

---

## Administration
- Take with or after food to reduce stomach upset.
- Use lowest effective dose for shortest time possible.
- Avoid alcohol and other NSAIDs.

---

## Contraindications
- Allergy to celecoxib, sulfonamides, or other NSAIDs
- Active peptic ulcer or GI bleeding
- Severe liver or kidney impairment
- Heart failure, ischemic heart disease, or stroke history
- Pregnancy (especially 3rd trimester)

---

## Adverse Reactions

**Common**
- Indigestion, stomach pain
- Headache, dizziness
- Oedema (swelling), raised blood pressure

**Serious / Rare**
- Heart attack or stroke (especially with prolonged use)
- GI ulcer or bleeding (less frequent than with non-selective NSAIDs)
- Liver enzyme increase
- Allergic skin reactions

---

## Pregnancy and Lactation

<Callout>
**Pregnancy:**
Avoid, especially in 3rd trimester.
May cause premature closure of fetal ductus arteriosus.

**Lactation:**
Excreted in small amounts in breast milk.
Avoid or use short-term with caution.
</Callout>

---

## Warnings
- Cardiovascular and renal risks increase with long-term use.
- Monitor blood pressure and renal function regularly.
- Avoid concurrent use with anticoagulants, corticosteroids, or other NSAIDs.
