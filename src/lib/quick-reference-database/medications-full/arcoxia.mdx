## Overview

**Arcoxia**, with the generic name **Etoricoxib**, is a selective COX-2 inhibitor, a type of non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID). It is used to treat various types of pain and inflammatory conditions.

### Key Points
- **Drug Class**: Selective COX-2 Inhibitor (NSAID)
- **Primary Use**: Pain and inflammation relief
- **Administration**: Oral (tablet)

---

## Indications

Arcoxia is indicated for the relief of symptoms in the treatment of:
- Osteoarthritis
- Rheumatoid arthritis
- Ankylosing spondylitis
- Acute gouty arthritis
- Acute pain, including postoperative pain

---

## Dosing

- **Osteoarthritis**: 60 mg once daily.
- **Rheumatoid Arthritis**: 90 mg once daily.
- **Acute Gout**: 120 mg once daily, used only for the acute painful period.
- **Postoperative Dental Pain**: 90 mg once daily, limited to a maximum of 3 days.

**Important**: The lowest effective dose should be used for the shortest possible duration.

---

## Contraindications

Do not use Arcoxia in patients with:
- Active peptic ulceration or active gastro-intestinal (GI) bleeding.
- Severe congestive heart failure.
- Inflammatory bowel disease.
- Ischemic heart disease, peripheral arterial disease, and/or cerebrovascular disease.

---

## Safety & Warnings

- **Cardiovascular Risk**: NSAIDs may cause an increased risk of serious cardiovascular thrombotic events, myocardial infarction, and stroke.
- **Gastrointestinal Risk**: NSAIDs cause an increased risk of serious GI adverse events including bleeding, ulceration, and perforation of the stomach or intestines.
- **Renal Effects**: Long-term administration of NSAIDs has resulted in renal papillary necrosis and other renal injury.

Always consult with a healthcare professional before starting or stopping any medication.
