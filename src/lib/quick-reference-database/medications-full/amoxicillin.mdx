<Callout>
  A beta-lactam antibiotic (aminopenicillin) commonly used for a wide range of
  bacterial infections. Use according to local susceptibility patterns and
  stewardship principles.
</Callout>

---

## Indication

- Respiratory tract infections, otitis media, sinusitis, skin and soft tissue infections, urinary tract infections (selected cases).

---

## Dose

### Adults

- Typical oral dosing: 500 mg every 8 hours or 875 mg every 12 hours depending on indication and severity. Duration guided by infection type.

### Paediatrics

- Weight-based dosing: commonly 25–50 mg/kg/day in divided doses; higher doses may be used for severe infections.

---

## Administration

- Take with or without food. Oral suspension should be reconstituted and dosed using the supplied measuring device.

---

## Contraindications

<Alert type="error">
  - History of penicillin hypersensitivity (immediate or delayed reactions).
</Alert>

Use with caution in patients with mononucleosis (risk of rash) and severe renal impairment (dose adjustments may be required).

---

## Adverse Reactions

- Common: diarrhoea, nausea, rash.
- Serious: severe hypersensitivity (anaphylaxis), Clostridioides difficile infection.

---

## Pregnancy and Lactation

- Generally considered safe in pregnancy and lactation when indicated; follow local guidance.
