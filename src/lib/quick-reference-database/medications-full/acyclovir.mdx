Acyclovir is an antiviral drug primarily used for the treatment of herpes simplex virus infections, chickenpox, and shingles. It works by slowing the growth and spread of the herpes virus in the body.

## Indications

- **Chickenpox (Varicella)**: Reduces the severity and duration of the illness.
- **Shingles (Herpes Zoster)**: Helps heal the skin and reduces pain.
- **Genital Herpes**: Used for both initial outbreaks and recurrent episodes.

## Contraindications

- Known hypersensitivity to acyclovir or valacyclovir.

## Adverse Effects

Common side effects may include:
- Nausea and vomiting
- Diarrhea
- Headache
- Malaise

## Pregnancy and Lactation

- **Pregnancy**: Generally considered safe, but use only when the potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the fetus.
- **Lactation**: Acyclovir is excreted in breast milk. Caution is advised.
