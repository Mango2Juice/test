# Eperisone (Myonal)

<Callout>
Centrally acting muscle relaxant.
Relieves muscle stiffness and pain by improving blood flow and reducing muscle spasm.
</Callout>

---

## Indication
- Muscle spasm due to spinal disease
- Muscle stiffness after stroke or injury
- Neck or shoulder pain
- Back pain

---

## Administration
- Take after meals to reduce stomach upset.
- Swallow whole with water.
- Do not double dose if missed.

---

## Contraindications
- Allergy to eperisone or other ingredients
- Severe liver impairment
- Pregnancy unless clearly needed

---

## Adverse Reactions

**Common**
- Drowsiness, dizziness
- Nausea, stomach discomfort
- Weakness

**Less common**
- Rash, itching
- Low blood pressure
- Liver enzyme elevation (rare)

---

## Pregnancy and Lactation

<Callout>
**Pregnancy:**
Use only if benefits outweigh risks.
Limited human data available.

**Lactation:**
Safety not well established.
Avoid or use with caution.
</Callout>
