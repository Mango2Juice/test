<Callout>
  Amoxicillin combined with clavulanic acid (beta-lactamase inhibitor) to
  broaden activity against beta-lactamase producing organisms. Dosing varies by
  formulation and indication; follow product labelling.
</Callout>

---

## Indication

- Respiratory, ENT, skin and soft tissue, and other infections where beta-lactamase producing organisms are suspected or proven.

---

## Dose

- Dosing depends on formulation and the amoxicillin component. Common adult regimens include 500/125 mg three times daily or 875/125 mg twice daily. For paediatrics use weight-based dosing of the amoxicillin component (commonly 25–45 mg/kg/day divided).

---

## Administration

- Take with food to improve absorption and reduce gastrointestinal upset.

---

## Contraindications

<Alert type="error">
  - Hypersensitivity to penicillins or clavulanic acid.
</Alert>

---

## Adverse Reactions

- Gastrointestinal upset (nausea, diarrhoea) and rash are common. Clostridioides difficile infection is a potential risk.

---

## Pregnancy and Lactation

- Generally acceptable if indicated; follow local guidance and product SmPC.
